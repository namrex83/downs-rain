Radar rain rates come from Bureau of Meteorology images, added up every few minutes. Each 9am the total is scaled to match the BOM gauges, then nudged locally near each gauge. Radar reads less reliably far from Toowoomba, behind hills and in hail. Satellite imagery © Esri and partners; street map © OpenStreetMap contributors.
